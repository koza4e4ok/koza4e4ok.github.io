Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Mail Brief · Last updated 22 October 2026

OpenClaw Mail Brief ("the app") is a personal tool operated by koza4e4ok ("the operator") for their own use only. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the Gmail read-only permission (gmail.readonly), and only for the operator's own Google account, the app reads only the sender, subject, date and labels (such as unread and important) of messages that arrived in the Primary inbox during the previous 24 hours. It does not read message bodies, previews or attachments, and it cannot send, delete, label or otherwise change any email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce a short daily summary that is sent to the operator's private Telegram chat. Before summarizing, the app hides the subject of security, sign-in, password-reset and financial emails, and masks number codes, account numbers and links in all other subjects. The filtered data is summarized by Google's Gemini model through the Gemini API.

Storage and retention

Email data is processed in memory and in temporary files on the operator's private server and is deleted when each daily run finishes. The app keeps no database of email content. The finished summary remains in the operator's Telegram chat and may appear in the server's application logs.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the service providers needed to produce and deliver the summary: Google (Gemini API) and Telegram. No humans other than the operator read the data.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer read any email.
