OpenClaw Mail Brief

A private, single-user morning digest of my own Gmail inbox.

OpenClaw Mail Brief is a personal tool that the operator (koza4e4ok) runs on their own server. Once a day it reads, with read-only access, only the sender, subject, date and labels of messages that arrived in the operator's Primary inbox during the previous 24 hours, and sends a short summary to the operator's private Telegram chat. It never reads message bodies, previews or attachments.

It is not a public service. It is not offered to other users, and it does not send, delete or change any email.
